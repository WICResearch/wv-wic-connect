FRN & Community Partner Referrals

Family Resource Networks, schools, healthcare organizations, and community partners can help families learn about WIC services and connect with their local clinic.

Our planned referral workflow will support partner attribution, family contact preferences, referral follow-up, and aggregate outreach reporting. Personal referrals will be accepted only after an approved secure intake process is configured.

Please do not enter or transmit participant names, telephone numbers, or other private information through GitHub Pages or public links.
